---
name: District boundary derived from ward union
description: Saligrama district polygon must be regenerated from ward polygons, never hand-edited independently
---

The Saligrama district boundary in `geofences.json` is not an independently-drawn polygon — it is generated by taking the geometric union (dissolve) of all ward polygons via `scripts/src/rebuild-district-boundary.ts` (uses `@turf/turf`).

**Why:** the district boundary used to be hand-digitized separately from the wards, which left ~5.76% of the district area not covered by any ward. Points in that gap hit an arbitrary `officers[0]` fallback (no `ORDER BY`) in `findOfficerForLocation`, causing reports to be silently misassigned to the wrong officer/ward. Regenerating the district as the ward union closes the gap by construction — any point that's in the district is guaranteed to be in some ward.

**How to apply:** if ward polygons in `geofences.json` are ever added, removed, or re-shaped, re-run `pnpm --filter @workspace/scripts exec tsx ./src/rebuild-district-boundary.ts` to regenerate the district polygon — do not hand-edit the district boundary directly, or the gap will reappear. Also keep the `findOfficerForLocation` nearest-ward fallback (distance-to-ring, not `officers[0]`) as a defensive measure for any residual floating-point edge cases at shared ward borders.
